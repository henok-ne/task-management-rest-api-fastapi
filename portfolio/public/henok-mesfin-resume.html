Profile

Self-taught full-stack developer working both halves of the stack: React and Tailwind on the front end, FastAPI or Next.js route handlers on the back, over PostgreSQL and MongoDB. My flagship project is EthioPayroll, a multi-tenant payroll and HR platform for Ethiopian businesses that I built on PostgreSQL with row-level security, then re-architected on MongoDB — so I have lived through the trade-offs of both data layers rather than just reading about them.

What I care about most is correctness at the edges: tax brackets that match the published ERCA schedule, a tenant boundary no query can leak across, and input that is sanitised before it reaches the database. I document what I build, and I ship it — Docker images, CI, and a live deployment.
